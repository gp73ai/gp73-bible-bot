Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Godsprisoner and Summit Ridge Media collect, use, and protect information submitted through our websites, free-resource forms, stores, memberships, and email communications.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, resource requested, acquisition source, campaign information, page activity connected to your request, and purchase or membership information when applicable. Payment details are processed by our checkout and payment providers and are not stored on this website.

How we use information

We use information to deliver requested resources, provide relevant teaching and product communications, maintain customer records, improve our content and offers, prevent abuse, and meet legal and operational obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers for website hosting, customer records, email delivery, automation, databases, checkout, and payment processing. They receive only the information needed to perform their services.

Email choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email. We may still send necessary transactional or customer-service messages related to a purchase or active membership.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for delivery, customer service, analytics, legal obligations, and legitimate business operations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact members@iamgodsprisoner.com. Some records may be retained when required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or transaction purposes.

Updates

We may update this policy as our services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
